# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Financial operators and merchants who package daily transaction/incoming transfer report files for bank/merchant submission. They work in fast-paced operational environments where data accuracy is critical.

## Product Purpose
ZipShift is a high-performance utility that groups CSV/PDF files by their prefix, subtracts one day from the date suffix in the filename, packages them into ZIPs, and downloads them. It operates 100% client-side in the browser for maximum security and data privacy.

## Brand Personality
Helpful, reliable, and clean. Approachable and professional, focusing on ease of use, absolute clarity, and calming light themes.

## Anti-references
Avoid typical crypto-neon on dark or SaaS clichés (no excessive glassmorphism, no nested cards, no side-stripe borders). Avoid cold, standard database/admin table layouts with no personality.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity Over Complexity**: Keep screens focused on the primary drag-and-drop and review workflows, reducing cognitive load.
- **Calm, High-Contrast Light Mode**: Use light, airy themes with soft, soothing colors (e.g. gentle blues/teals/emeralds) that prioritize legibility under standard office lighting.
- **Immediate, Contextual Feedback**: Provide visual confirmation for file validations, grouping, and zipping statuses immediately.
- **Secure by Default**: Highlight that no files leave the client's device, fostering a sense of security and trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. Focus on high color contrast for text, robust keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, and descriptive, screen-reader-friendly status updates.
